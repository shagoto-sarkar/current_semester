RESEARCH ETHICS
Honesty: Data, Result, Methodology, Reporting,
No fabrication, No Misinterpretation.

Integrity: Keeping Promises, Consistency of
thought and flow, Sincerity.

Carefulness: Critically and Carefully Analyze the
works, Avoid negligence, Avoid errors.

Objectivity: Avoid Biasness, in design, about
data, peer review, grant approval, grant
submission, testimony

Respect Intellectual Property: Giving Credit, Not
using unpublished data without proper approval,
Honor Patents, Copyrights, Literature Findings,
Never Plagiarize


RESEARCH ETHICS
 Communicate:

Publish for advancement of research,

No duplication
 Confidentiality: Protect trade secrets, Patent Records,
Protect confidential communications
 Openness: Share data, tools, codes, resources. Open to
new advancements and constructive criticism
 Non-Discriminative: Avoid Discrimination based on
race, religion, ethnicity, sex, orientation, appearance
or any other factor not related to scientific competence
& Integrity.
 Social Responsibility: To Promote social harmony
rather harm to the society. Using research, education,
findings on that manner
